# What's new

What changed in the portal, newest week first. The portal shows this file under
What's new in the user menu and Ctrl K, so write for the people who use it, not
for developers: one line per change, plain words, no PR numbers. Leave out
changes nobody can see (dependencies, lint, tests).

Format: a `## Week of <Month> <day>, <year>` heading (the Monday), then one
`- **Area:** change` line per change, each on a single line, with no other
Markdown (the dialog shows it as plain text). The tests check this.

## Week of November 16, 2026

- **What's new:** this list, in the user menu and Ctrl K. A dot on your photo means there's something you haven't seen.
- **@mentions:** type @ in a task description, comment, lead note or lead update to mention a teammate. They get a notification.
- **Comments:** the portal asks before it throws away a comment or lead update you haven't posted.
- **Editor:** nested bullets, with Indent and Outdent in the list menu or Tab and Shift+Tab. Pasted Markdown turns into formatting, and comments keep highlights, checklists and headings.
- **Boards:** select cards with Shift-click or ⌘-click, then set an assignee, set a due date, archive or drag them together.
- **Boards:** right-click a task for Set due date, Send to top and Send to bottom. Leads have Send to top and Send to bottom too.
- **Boards:** Mark as done on task cards, and you can drag the next card while the last one is still saving.
- **Notifications:** you hear about what others do on your tasks and leads, not your own changes. Archive replaces Clear, so nothing is lost.
- **Help:** Get help in the user menu and Ctrl K shows who built the portal and how to reach them.
- **Reference:** one page with tabs for System health, roles and permissions, notifications, emails, PDFs and chat messages.
- **Account:** your profile, connections and preferences open in one sheet from the user menu.
- **Settings:** a Business tab for the name, logo, address, email senders and time zone. Invoices show the business address and phone.
- **Integrations:** if Google, GitHub, Vercel or Supabase stops accepting your connection, the portal tells you and offers Reconnect.
- **Companies:** the "+N" after a company's tags shows the hidden tags.
- **Updates:** an open portal reloads itself after a new release, so nobody works on an old version.
- **Fixes:** saving a lead no longer reloads the page, links to archived tasks open, and comments and updates written by AI show "via Claude".

## Week of November 9, 2026

- **Boards:** task and lead boards sit in the page frame with a filter bar, and use status lanes. Dragging is smoother.
- **Ctrl K:** search every record and open its sheet.
- **Tags:** add reusable tags to companies.
- **Lists:** sort any column. Users and Clients have their own pages.
- **Sheets:** warn before you save over someone else's change, and show who else has the record open. Undo and redo work in every sheet.
- **Sheets:** opening a record that's already open goes back to it instead of opening it twice.
- **Right-click menus:** set the assignee from the menu.
- **Phones:** a one-row toolbar and a menu button.
- **Invoices:** the paid alert posts as soon as Stripe confirms payment.
- **Fixes:** dark mode stays on after a refresh, and avatar photos load at once.
