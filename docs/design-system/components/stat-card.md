# Stat card, info panel and billing type badge

**StatCard** (`apps/internal/components/ui/stat-card.tsx`): one figure with its label above it, in a small tinted card (`bg-muted/40`, border, `px-4 py-3`). Set two or more side by side in a grid (`grid grid-cols-2 gap-3`). Used for the time log dialog's project and client hours. An hours balance (a client's remaining prepaid hours) takes `tone={balanceTone(hours)}` (`lib/billing/balance-tone.ts`): muted green (`text-success/85`) while hours are left, muted red (`text-destructive/85`) once overdrawn, neutral at zero. Every "remaining" figure uses it: the company sheet's Hours, the project overview, the time log panel and the update composer's Hours card (`BALANCE_TONE_CLASS` for plain text). Hours keep the `1.5h` format (`formatHours`). (guard: review)

**BillingTypeBadge** (`apps/internal/components/billing/billing-type-badge.tsx`): a client's billing type as a badge, Prepaid (green) or Net 30 (amber), the billing type picker's colours from `CLIENT_BILLING_TYPE_SELECT_OPTIONS`, in the one badge family. Put it right after the client's name; never a "Billing" label row. Used in the invoices list and the time log dialog. (guard: review)

**InfoPanel** (`apps/internal/components/ui/info-panel.tsx`): read-only context above a form, so it never reads as part of it: one flat tinted panel (`bg-muted/50`, no border, unlike the bordered inputs), `InfoPanelBody` for names and badges, `InfoPanelStats` for figures in cells split by hairlines, then `InfoPanelDivider` (a full-width hairline with 24px either side) before the first field. The time log dialog uses it: the client (with its billing badge) and project by their coloured icons, no labels, then the project's and client's hours. StatCard stays for figures that stand on their own (the company sheet's Hours). (guard: review)
