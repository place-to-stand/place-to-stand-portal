# ContextMenu

The right-click menu, shadcn/ui's Context Menu (base-nova, on Base UI). From `@pts/ui/context-menu`. It opens at the pointer on a right click, or on a long press on touch.

**Look:** the same surface and items as `DropdownMenu`: `bg-popover border rounded-md p-1 shadow-popover`, 16px muted icons, `variant="destructive"` items in `destructive`.

## RecordContextMenu

`components/table/record-context-menu.tsx`: the one right-click menu on every record row and board card. It renders the row or card itself as the trigger (`render`), so no wrapper lands between a table body and its rows.

**Items, in order:** Edit (opens the record, as a click on the row does) · Duplicate (only for `DUPLICABLE_SHEET_ENTITIES`: tasks, leads, invoices, hour blocks) · Set assignee › (tasks and leads, see below) · Set due date › (tasks) · Mark as done (task board cards) · separator · Send to top, Send to bottom (board cards) · separator · Archive, or Restore on an archive list. When archive or restore can't run, the item is disabled and the reason shows under it. The items mount only while the menu is open, so rows and cards don't re-render on URL changes.

**Rules**

- Every record row and board card answers a right click with `RecordContextMenu`; lists pass the handlers their row buttons already use, so the menu never runs a second version of an action. (guard: review)
- Duplicate opens the record's create sheet filled in from it (`?<entity>=copy-<uuid>`, `useSheetParams().duplicate`). Saving creates a new record and never touches the source. Workflow position resets: a copied task starts in the first column and a copied lead at the first stage. (guard: review)
- Companies, people, submissions and projects have no Duplicate: a second "Acme" is a mistake, submissions come from the website, and a project's tasks wouldn't come with it. (guard: review)
- The menu offers only what the row already offers: `editable={false}` where the list doesn't open the record (archived time logs), `duplicable={false}` for a viewer who can't create it, and no `onArchive` where the list can't drop an archived record (a lead sheet's tasks). (guard: review)
- `copy-<uuid>` is a valid param value only for duplicable entities; anywhere else it is ignored, and `/api/sheets/init` takes record ids only. (guard: review)
- Draggable cards pass `draggable`, so on touch a held finger drags and only a right click opens the menu (the touch timer and Android's touch `contextmenu` event are both cancelled). (guard: review)
- Board cards with no archive flow of their own use `useRecordArchive` (`components/table/use-record-archive.tsx`): the shared `ArchiveConfirmDialog`, the action, toasts and a refresh. (guard: review)
- **Set assignee** (`assign`, `components/table/assignee-submenu.tsx`) is on task and lead cards for people who can edit them. Hovering it opens a submenu to the side: the people who can take the record (the same rule a save checks, from `GET /api/tasks/[id]/assignee-options` or `/api/leads/assignee-options`), each with their avatar and the current one checked on the right, then a separator and **Clear assignee** (disabled when there is none). A pick saves at once (`setTaskAssignee` / `setLeadAssignee`, the same notification and activity row as a sheet save) and shows a toast, "Assigned to Kris" or "Assignee cleared", with the record's name as its link. (guard: review)
- **Set due date** (`dueDate`, `components/table/due-date-submenu.tsx`) is on task cards for people who can edit them. Its submenu offers Today, Tomorrow and Next Monday (counted in the company's time zone, as the card's "Due today" is), then a calendar for any other day (the current date selected; it keeps its own arrow keys), then **Clear due date** (disabled when there is none). A pick shows on the card at once (`lib/tasks/optimistic-due-on.ts`), saves through `changeTaskDueDate` (the same activity row as a sheet save) and toasts "Due Nov 18, 2026" or "Due date cleared" with the task as its link; a failure puts the old date back. (guard: review)
- **Send to top / Send to bottom** (`move`) are on board cards (project board, My Tasks, leads board) for people who can reorder that board. Each is the same move as dragging the card to that end of its column, saved the same way (the task's rank on a project board, the person's own order on My Tasks, the lead's rank on the leads board), so it is instant and rolls back on failure. The cards slide to their new places (`useFlipOnMove`, skipped for reduced motion) and the column doesn't scroll after the card (board columns set `overflow-anchor: none`); a toast, "Moved to top" or "Moved to bottom", names the task or lead (`move.name`). The item is disabled when the card is already there. Lists that aren't boards (a lead sheet's tasks) leave them out. With a search or the Done window on, "top" is the top of the cards shown, as a drag would place it. (guard: review)
- **Mark as done** (`markDone`) is on task board cards (project board, My Tasks) for people who can reorder that board. It is the same move as dragging the card to the top of Done (`useMarkDone`, `components/board/use-mark-done.ts`; the move itself is `markDoneMove` in `lib/dnd/mark-done.ts`), saved the same way, so it is instant and rolls back on failure. On a selected card it marks every selected card, as a drag of it would carry them; cards already in Done stay put. A toast says "Marked as done" with the task as its link, or "Marked 3 tasks as done". The item is disabled on a card (or selection) already done. Lists that aren't boards leave it out. (guard: review)
