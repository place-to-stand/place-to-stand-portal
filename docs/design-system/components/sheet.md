# Sheet

The right-side panel where every entity is created and edited, on Base UI Dialog. From `apps/internal/components/ui/sheet.tsx`.

**Look:** `bg-background shadow-lg border-l`, full width on phones and `sm:w-3/4` above, capped by `size` (`sm` · `md` · `lg` · `xl` · `2xl` · `wide` sm:max-w-4xl lg:max-w-5xl · `xwide` · `full`); backdrop `bg-black/50`; slide in and out 300ms. `SheetHeader` is `bg-muted/50 p-4 gap-0.5`, `SheetTitle` font-semibold, `SheetDescription` text-sm muted; `SheetFooter` `mt-auto justify-end gap-3 p-4`. Close X top-right at 70% opacity.

**Header:** entity sheets use `SheetFormHeader` (`components/sheets/sheet-form-header.tsx`), not a hand-built `SheetHeader`: one compact title row (`p-2.5`, `text-base`) with its own close button (pass `hideCloseButton` to `SheetContent`) and a 2px bottom rule in the entity's colour (`ENTITY_ACCENTS.<entity>.sheetHeader`, from the entity registry: see **Entities** in the README). Client is a state of a company, not an entity: the Client badge, the Clients filters, the Billing tab's state and the client rows on Projects use the client briefcase (`ENTITY_ICONS.client`, `ENTITY_ACCENTS.client`). A sign-in is part of the person and has no icon of its own. No eyebrow or description above the fold. The registry's `icon` class tints an entity's icon in list cells (e.g. the invoice icon on the Invoices list).

**Opening:** a sheet is addressed by one query param (`?task=<uuid>`, `?client=new`) through `useSheetParams`/`useSheetParamSelection` — never a route segment, never a hand-written URL; build links with `lib/sheets/hrefs.ts`. `open` comes from the hook's local mirror, not the raw param.

**Rules**
- Save is always enabled; disable only while saving or on a real validation failure. Never gate on `isDirty`.
- Save = done = close: a successful save closes the sheet, for create and edit alike. A create sheet never becomes an edit sheet.
- **Only Save writes.** Nothing inside a sheet saves on click, toggle, switch, star, drag or blur: those change the form, and Save applies them. Closing with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?".
- **Instant actions are their own records or act outside the app**, so they save on their own click: comments, lead updates, logging time (its dialog), invoice email / mark as sent / revert to draft / void, acknowledging a submission, drafting a client update, planning (start, generate, dispatch, cancel), and sending a password reset. Sending an invoice is blocked while it has unsaved edits. Uploads (photos, attachments) go to a staging path at once and are claimed on Save.
- Focus lands on the field marked `data-autofocus`, else the popup (not the close button).
- A two-column sheet (form plus a side column, like the lead and invoice sheets) stacks below `lg`: the form, then the side column, in one scroll with the footer pinned to the bottom. Side by side, the form column is too narrow to use under `lg`.
- **Create and edit show the same sheet**, at the same size. Anything that needs the new record's id (a company's people, billing and portal access; a person's sign-in; a task's assignee and attachments) is collected in the form and written in the same Save. Only what can't exist before the record stays edit-only: activity and history, created/updated dates, archive/restore/delete, share links. Instant records (a lead's tasks and updates, a task's time logs and comments) keep their `SheetSection` heading on create over an `EmptyState` ("Add tasks after you save the lead."); a section that can't apply yet (a lead's conversion) stays hidden.
- One Save, one transaction: everything the sheet's form writes (the record plus any company or person it finds or creates) commits together or not at all, and activity rows are written after the commit.
- Presses inside toasts or other popups (select, dropdown) don't count as outside presses.
- **Related records stack.** Opening a person, company, lead or task from inside a sheet opens its own sheet on top (the URL keeps the stack order, `?company=…&contact=…`); closing it goes back to the sheet underneath. Never navigate away or replace the sheet you're in. Small focused tasks (link a repo, email an invoice, log time) may open a dialog over a sheet.
- A single record linked to this sheet's entity (an hour block's invoice) renders as a `LinkedRecordCard` (`components/sheets/linked-record-card.tsx`): its name on top, an "Open …" link to the other record's list page with its sheet open (`lib/sheets/hrefs.ts`), and label/value details below a rule.
