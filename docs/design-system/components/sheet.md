# Sheet

The right-side panel where every entity is created and edited, on Base UI Dialog. From `apps/internal/components/ui/sheet.tsx`.

**Look:** `bg-background shadow-lg border-l`, full width on phones and `sm:w-3/4` above, capped by one of two sizes (below); backdrop `bg-black/50`; slide in and out 300ms. `SheetHeader` is `bg-muted/50 p-4 gap-0.5`, `SheetTitle` font-semibold, `SheetDescription` text-sm muted; `SheetFooter` `mt-auto justify-end gap-3 p-4`. Close X top-right at 70% opacity.

**Header:** entity sheets use `SheetFormHeader` (`components/sheets/sheet-form-header.tsx`), not a hand-built `SheetHeader`: one compact title row (`p-2.5`, `text-base`): the entity's icon (the default `leading`; a person shows their avatar), then the title, which is the record's name when editing ("Acme Industries", "Invoice INV-0142", "Hour block for Acme") and "Create <entity>" when creating, never "Edit <entity>"; with its own close button (pass `hideCloseButton` to `SheetContent`) and a 2px bottom rule in the entity's colour (`ENTITY_ACCENTS.<entity>.sheetHeader`, from the entity registry: see **Entities** in the README). Client is a state of a company, not an entity: the Client badge, the Clients filters, the Billing tab's state and the client rows on Projects use the client briefcase (`ENTITY_ICONS.client`, `ENTITY_ACCENTS.client`). A sign-in is part of the person and has no icon of its own. No eyebrow or description above the fold. The registry's `icon` class tints an entity's icon in list cells (e.g. the invoice icon on the Invoices list).

**The layout sets the width.** `columns={1}` (672px, the narrowest that fits the full rich text toolbar on one line) for a one-column sheet: company, person, task, project, hour block, submission, template, the phone filters. `columns={2}` (768px below `lg`, a set 1184px from `lg`) for a form beside a side column: lead, invoice; the side column is `SHEET_SIDE_COLUMN_CLASS` (384px) and stacks below `lg`. Nothing in between, and no per-sheet `max-w-*` override. Phones get the full screen. The one exception is the task sheet's planning panel, which widens the sheet while it's open.

**One tab style.** A sheet with tabs uses `SheetTabs` (`components/sheets/sheet-tabs.tsx`) under the header, mirrored to `?tab=`; never a second, inner set of tabs. Everything else is a `SheetSection` in one scroll. **The bottom of a sheet is fixed: comments or updates, then Activity, last.** The task sheet runs its fields, Time logs, Comments, then Activity; the lead sheet's side column runs Conversion, Tasks, then Updates.

**Activity is collapsed.** A sheet that shows a record's history uses `SheetActivitySection` (`components/sheets/sheet-activity-section.tsx`), never a hand-built `SheetSection` around `ActivityFeed`: a closed accordion headed "Activity (12)" with a chevron (a button with `aria-expanded`; the open panel is a `region` named by it), on the `@pts/ui` Collapsible. The count is one cheap request (`GET /api/activity?count=1`, the feed's filters and access rules, a `count(*)`); the entries load only when it's opened, and unmount when it's closed. Edit only: a create sheet has no Activity. Today only the task sheet shows one.

**Opening:** a sheet is addressed by one query param (`?task=<uuid>`, `?client=new`) through `useSheetParams`/`useSheetParamSelection` — never a route segment, never a hand-written URL; build links with `lib/sheets/hrefs.ts`. `open` comes from the hook's local mirror, not the raw param.

**Rules**
- Save is always enabled; disable only while saving or on a real validation failure. Never gate on `isDirty`.
- **Save results are toasts.** Success ("Company saved", "Company created") and failure ("Unable to save company", the reason in the description) show as a toast on every sheet, with no inline error box; a failed save keeps the sheet open with its edits. Field errors (required, format) stay under their field (`FormMessage`).
- Save = done = close: a successful save closes the sheet, for create and edit alike. A create sheet never becomes an edit sheet.
- **Say what Save does.** A save side effect (an email sent, a sign-in created, billing started or stopped, someone added to a company) shows as one caution alert, `SaveSideEffect` (see `alert.md`), never a muted line.
- **Only Save writes.** Nothing inside a sheet saves on click, toggle, switch, star, drag or blur: those change the form, and Save applies them. Closing with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?".
- **Instant actions are their own records or act outside the app**, so they save on their own click: comments, lead updates, logging time (its dialog), invoice email / mark as sent / revert to draft / void, acknowledging a submission, drafting a client update, planning (start, generate, dispatch, cancel), and sending a password reset. Sending an invoice is blocked while it has unsaved edits. Uploads (photos, attachments) go to a staging path at once and are claimed on Save.
- Focus lands on the field marked `data-autofocus`, else the popup (not the close button).
- A two-column sheet (form plus a side column, like the lead and invoice sheets) stacks below `lg`: the form, then the side column, in one scroll with the footer pinned to the bottom. Side by side, the form column is too narrow to use under `lg`.
- **Create and edit show the same sheet**, at the same size. Anything that needs the new record's id (a company's people, billing and portal access; a person's sign-in; a task's assignee and attachments) is collected in the form and written in the same Save. Only what can't exist before the record stays edit-only: activity and history, created/updated dates, archive/delete, share links. Instant records (a lead's tasks and updates, a task's time logs and comments) keep their `SheetSection` heading on create over an `EmptyState` ("Add tasks after you save the lead."); a section that can't apply yet (a lead's conversion) stays hidden.
- One Save, one transaction: everything the sheet's form writes (the record plus any company or person it finds or creates) commits together or not at all, and activity rows are written after the commit.
- **No silent overwrites (person sheet).** Save sends the person's `updated_at` as the sheet loaded it; if someone saved them since, Save is refused with a toast ("Someone else saved this person. Reload to see their changes.") and nothing is written. Anything that changes what the person sheet shows (their details, companies, titles, primary, portal access, sign-in, role) bumps `contacts.updated_at`.
- **Restore lives on the archive list only.** A sheet never offers Restore. An archived record opened in a sheet is read-only, with one muted line at the top ("Archived. Restore it from the archive list.") and no archive action in the footer.
- Presses inside toasts or other popups (select, dropdown) don't count as outside presses.
- **Related records stack.** Opening a person, company, lead or task from inside a sheet opens its own sheet on top (the URL keeps the stack order, `?company=…&contact=…`); closing it goes back to the sheet underneath. Open it with `useSheetParams().open(entity, id)`, passing `{ tab: 'billing' }` to land on a tab, never with a `*SheetHref` link or `router.push`: those leave the page. Never navigate away or replace the sheet you're in; a link to a page (a project board, the update composer) is the only way out. A page can open the same way (winning a lead on the board opens its company's Billing tab over the board). Small focused tasks (link a repo, email an invoice, log time) may open a dialog over a sheet.
- **Linked records are rows.** Every record listed in a sheet (a company's people, leads and projects, a converted lead's company, person and projects, an hour block's invoice, an invoice's hour blocks) is a `SheetRecordRow` from `components/sheets/entity-record-rows.tsx`: `ContactRecordRow`, `CompanyRecordRow`, `LeadRecordRow`, `ProjectRecordRow`, `InvoiceRecordRow`, `HourBlockRecordRow`, inside a `SheetRecordList`. A row with a sheet opens it on top (`onOpen`); a project row links to its board (`href`), and one without a slug is plain text. Callers add only a note and actions.
- **Record rows with fields.** A record row that holds form fields (a person's title and portal access in the company sheet's People section and the person sheet's Companies list, a closer's share on the Billing tab) keeps its first line as the normal preview (icon, name, detail line, row actions) and puts the fields under it inside the same card, one per line: pass them as `fields`, each a `RecordRowField` (`components/sheets/sheet-record-row.tsx`) with a visible label on its left ("Title" [input], then "Portal access" [switch]; "Share" [input]). Labels share one width and fields keep one order, so they line up from card to card. A field's visible label is its label: no placeholder standing in for it, no status tooltip on a switch; keep an `aria-label` that names the record ("Portal access for Ada Lovelace").
