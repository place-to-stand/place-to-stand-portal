# Sheet

The right-side panel where every entity is created and edited, on Base UI Dialog. From `apps/internal/components/ui/sheet.tsx`.

**Look:** `bg-background shadow-lg border-l`, full width on phones and `sm:w-3/4` above, capped by one of two sizes (below); backdrop `bg-black/50`; slide in and out 300ms. `SheetHeader` is `bg-muted/50 p-4 gap-0.5`, `SheetTitle` font-semibold, `SheetDescription` text-sm muted; `SheetFooter` `mt-auto justify-end gap-3 p-4`. Close X top-right at 70% opacity.

**Header:** entity sheets use `SheetFormHeader` (`components/sheets/sheet-form-header.tsx`), not a hand-built `SheetHeader`: one compact title row (`p-2.5`, `text-base`): the entity's icon (the default `leading`; a person shows their avatar), then the title, which is the record's name when editing ("Acme Industries", "Invoice INV-0142", "Hour block for Acme") and "Create <entity>" when creating, never "Edit <entity>"; with its own close button (pass `hideCloseButton` to `SheetContent`) and a 2px bottom rule in the entity's colour (`ENTITY_ACCENTS.<entity>.sheetHeader`, from the entity registry: see **Entities** in the README). Client is a state of a company, not an entity: the Client badge, the Clients filters, the company sheet's Client column (Bill this company) and the client rows on Projects use the client briefcase (`ENTITY_ICONS.client`, `ENTITY_ACCENTS.client`). A sign-in is part of the person and has no icon of its own. No eyebrow or description above the fold. The registry's `icon` class tints an entity's icon in list cells (e.g. the invoice icon on the Invoices list).

**The layout sets the width.** `columns={1}` (720px: fits the full rich text toolbar on one line, with room to spare) for a one-column sheet: person, task, project, hour block, submission, template, the phone filters. `columns={2}` (768px below `lg`, a set 1184px from `lg`) for a form beside a side column: company, lead, invoice; the side column takes a named split from `SHEET_SIDE_COLUMN_CLASSES`: `default` (384px, the invoice) or `even` (480px, the lead, whose tasks and updates get worked in as much as the form, and the company, whose Client column holds the billing editor and Projects; the form keeps about 700px so the full rich text toolbar still fits on one line). No other widths. The side column stacks below `lg`. Nothing in between, and no per-sheet `max-w-*` override. Phones get the full screen. The one exception is the task sheet's planning panel, which widens the sheet while it's open.

**Focus lands on the first field.** Opening any sheet, create or edit, puts the cursor in its first field so you can just type (a new task: Title); on edit the caret goes to the end of the text. `SheetContent` does it for every sheet: a field marked `data-autofocus` wins, else the first enabled input, textarea, editor or picker trigger outside the header (read-only and disabled fields are skipped). Fields that render after the sheet's data loads take focus when they appear, unless focus has already moved (a click, a dialog).

**Section heading icons.** A section may lead its title with an icon: `SheetSection icon={…}`. An entity's registry icon (Tasks, Time logs, People, Projects, Invoices, Hour blocks) shows in that entity's colour automatically; a non-entity icon stays muted and comes from `CONCEPT_ICONS` (`lib/concept-icons.ts`: comment `MessageCircle`, attachment `Paperclip`, activity `History`), the same icon the task cards use for that concept. Never grey an entity icon by hand. The company and person sheets' sections (People, Leads; Companies) show no heading icon: leave `icon` off there. (guard: review)

**List sections sit tight.** A section that holds a list or an empty state (any section with a +, and Comments) puts its heading 8px above the content, the same gap as a field's label above its control; `SheetSection` does it when it has an `action` or `list`. A section of fields keeps 16px. (guard: review)

**One add button, one related list.** Every sheet section's add action (Add person, Add company, Add line item, Add task, Add update, Log time, Add member, Link repository, Create project) comes from `EntityList` (`components/sheets/entity-list.tsx`, see `entity-list.md`): the + alone, a ghost icon button on the section's title row, its full label the accessible name, with the list and a clickable empty state under it. Never a labelled outline button or a hand-built + beside a section title. (guard: lint)

**No generic first heading.** A sheet's first group of fields has no "Details" heading: it only restates the sheet. Leave `SheetSection`'s `title` out. Headings name real groups (Billing, Commission, People). (guard: review)

**Dividers between sections, except around a few boxed lists.** Sheet sections are separated by a full-width `Separator` in a `gap-6` column. A few EntityList sections drop the line: company People and Leads, person Companies, invoice Line items. Stacked sections with no line between them sit 24px apart, the same as one field to the next (`SHEET_SECTIONS_CLASS`: the company's Company column, the lead's Tasks and Updates). Invoice Line items alone takes `SHEET_BLOCK_CLASS`, 48px around it. The first group of fields never gets a line above it. (guard: review)

**The activity stream is last.** Updates, Comments, and then the collapsed Activity end the sheet: on a two-column sheet they are the last sections of the right column, below projects, tasks and everything else. (guard: review)

**One tab style.** A sheet with tabs uses `SheetTabs` (`components/sheets/sheet-tabs.tsx`) under the header, mirrored to `?tab=`; never a second, inner set of tabs. No sheet needs tabs today: everything is a `SheetSection` in one scroll, or in two columns. **The company sheet is two columns, no tabs:** Company on the left (details, People, Leads, Updates) and Client on the right (`even` split): the **Bill this company** switch is always first. Switched on, the billing fields show under it (the effective month, terms, commission, the save cautions), then Projects as record rows with Create project; switching it on for a company that isn't billed only changes the form (Save makes it a client, on create too); a former client's switch sits under one line with its last billing type ("Former client, last billed Net 30. Billing stopped."). No empty-state panel and no separate Make a client button. Sales sees the Client column read-only (where the company stands, no controls). Old links `?tab=billing`, `?tab=projects` and `?tab=people` open the sheet scrolled to that section; `?tab=details` or none is the top. **The bottom of a sheet is fixed: comments or updates, then Activity, last.** The task sheet runs its fields, Time logs, Comments, then Activity; the lead sheet's side column runs Conversion, Tasks, then Updates.

**Activity is collapsed.** A sheet that shows a record's history uses `SheetActivitySection` (`components/sheets/sheet-activity-section.tsx`), never a hand-built `SheetSection` around `ActivityFeed`: a closed accordion headed "Activity (12)" with a chevron on the house Accordion (`@pts/ui/accordion`, see `accordion.md`). The count is one cheap request (`GET /api/activity?count=1`, the feed's filters and access rules, a `count(*)`); the entries load only when it's opened, and unmount when it's closed. Edit only: a create sheet has no Activity. Today only the task sheet shows one.

**Opening:** a sheet is addressed by one query param (`?task=<uuid>`, `?client=new`) through `useSheetParams`/`useSheetParamSelection` — never a route segment, never a hand-written URL; build links with `lib/sheets/hrefs.ts`. `open` comes from the hook's local mirror, not the raw param.

**Rules**
- Save is always enabled; disable only while saving or on a real validation failure. Never gate on `isDirty`.
- **Save results are toasts.** Success ("Company saved", "Company created") and failure ("Unable to save company", the reason in the description) show as a toast on every sheet, with no inline error box; a failed save keeps the sheet open with its edits. Field errors (required, format) stay under their field (`FormMessage`).
- Save = done = close: a successful save closes the sheet, for create and edit alike. A create sheet never becomes an edit sheet.
- **Say what Save does.** A save side effect (an email sent, a sign-in created, billing started or stopped, someone added to a company) shows as one caution alert, `SaveSideEffect` (see `alert.md`), never a muted line.
- **Only Save writes.** Nothing inside a sheet saves on click, toggle, switch, star, drag or blur: those change the form, and Save applies them. Closing with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?".
- **Instant actions are their own records or act outside the app**, so they save on their own click: comments, lead updates, logging time (its dialog), invoice email / mark as sent / revert to draft / void, acknowledging a submission, drafting a client update, planning (start, generate, dispatch, cancel), and sending a password reset. Sending an invoice is blocked while it has unsaved edits. Uploads (photos, attachments) go to a staging path at once and are claimed on Save.
- Focus lands on the field marked `data-autofocus`, else the popup (not the close button).
- A two-column sheet (form plus a side column, like the lead and invoice sheets) stacks below `lg`: the form, then the side column, in one scroll with the footer pinned to the bottom. Side by side, the form column is too narrow to use under `lg`.
- **Create and edit show the same sheet**, at the same size. Anything that needs the new record's id (a company's people, billing and portal access; a person's sign-in; a task's assignee and attachments) is collected in the form and written in the same Save. Only what can't exist before the record stays edit-only: activity and history, created/updated dates, archive/delete, share links. Instant records (a lead's tasks and updates, a task's time logs and comments) keep their `SheetSection` heading on create over an `EmptyState` ("Add tasks after you save the lead."); a section that can't apply yet (a lead's conversion) stays hidden.
- One Save, one transaction: everything the sheet's form writes (the record plus any company or person it finds or creates) commits together or not at all, and activity rows are written after the commit.
- **No silent overwrites (person sheet).** Save sends the person's `updated_at` as the sheet loaded it; if someone saved them since, Save is refused with a toast ("Someone else saved this person. Reload to see their changes.") and nothing is written. Anything that changes what the person sheet shows (their details, companies, titles, primary, portal access, sign-in, role) bumps `contacts.updated_at`.
- **Restore lives on the archive list only.** A sheet never offers Restore. An archived record opened in a sheet is read-only, with one muted line at the top ("Archived. Restore it from the archive list.") and no archive action in the footer.
- Presses inside toasts or other popups (select, dropdown) don't count as outside presses.
- **Related records stack.** Opening a person, company, lead or task from inside a sheet opens its own sheet on top (the URL keeps the stack order, `?company=…&contact=…`); closing it goes back to the sheet underneath. Open it with `useSheetParams().open(entity, id)`, passing `{ tab: 'billing' }` to land on a section (the company sheet scrolls to its billing), never with a `*SheetHref` link or `router.push`: those leave the page. Never navigate away or replace the sheet you're in; a link to a page (a project board, the update composer) is the only way out. A page can open the same way (winning a lead on the board opens its company's sheet at its billing over the board). Small focused tasks (link a repo, email an invoice, log time) may open a dialog over a sheet.
- **Linked records are rows.** Every record listed in a sheet (a company's people, leads and projects, a converted lead's company, person and projects, an hour block's invoice, an invoice's hour blocks) is a `SheetRecordRow` from `components/sheets/entity-record-rows.tsx`: `ContactRecordRow`, `CompanyRecordRow`, `LeadRecordRow`, `ProjectRecordRow`, `InvoiceRecordRow`, `HourBlockRecordRow`, inside a `SheetRecordList`. A row with a sheet opens it on top (`onOpen`); a project row links to its board (`href`), and one without a slug is plain text. Callers add only a note and actions.
- **Record rows with fields (label and value).** A record row that holds form fields (a person's title and portal access in the company sheet's People section, a person's title at each company in the person sheet's Companies list, a closer's share in the company's Client column) keeps its first line as the normal preview (entity icon in its colour, name and detail line, row actions), then a hairline, then one label / value row per field: pass them as `fields`, each a `RecordRowField` (`components/sheets/sheet-record-row.tsx`). Labels sit in one 112px column so they line up from card to card; values start on the same edge. Text fields use `RECORD_ROW_INPUT_CLASS`: they read as plain text until hovered or focused, when the input box appears; never a boxed input inside the boxed card. A text field takes `grow` (fills the value column, up to 288px); a switch or short input keeps its own width. Every row is 32px tall. A field's visible label is its label: no placeholder standing in for it (a placeholder like "Add title" may show the empty state); keep an `aria-label` that names the record ("Portal access for Ada Lovelace"); no status tooltip on a switch.
- **Person access (person sheet).** One **Access** section answers "what can this person get into?": a `SegmentedControl` (`@pts/ui/segmented-control`, see `switch.md`) of **No access** / **Client portal** / **Team**, then one plain sentence ("Signs in to the client portal as ada@acme.com · sees Acme and Beta.", "Signs in to this portal as an Admin: everything.", "Can’t sign in."). Client portal's sentence names the sign-in email (their email: no email, no sign-in); which client companies they see is each company card's **Portal access** switch in the Companies section (under Title; off and disabled, with a hint, until Access is Client portal), as on the company sheet's People cards. The primary contact star sits in the card's top right, beside the ×, where the company sheet's People cards put theirs; Team shows Role (Admin, Sales) and, for a role change, "Role starts". It is a view of what Save already writes: the sign-in on or off and its role (`users`, `user_role_terms`) and each membership's `portal_access`. Staff (at the staff company) get No access or Team. A new sign-in, or a live one turned off, shows a `SaveSideEffect` ("Ada Lovelace will get a client portal sign-in (ada@acme.com) and an email link when you save.", "Ada Lovelace won’t be able to sign in once you save."). Sales sees the saved state and its sentence with the choice locked ("Only an admin can change someone’s sign-in."), without which companies a client sees. The Companies list only notes "Portal access" on a client row; the company sheet's People section keeps its Portal access switch as a shortcut to the same setting. Built from `components/contacts/person-access-choice.tsx`; `/design#access`.
