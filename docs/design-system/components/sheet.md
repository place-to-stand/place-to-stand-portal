# Sheet

The right-side panel where every entity is created and edited, on Base UI Dialog. From `apps/internal/components/ui/sheet.tsx`.

**Look:** `bg-background shadow-lg border-l`, full width on phones and `sm:w-3/4` above, capped by one of two sizes (below); backdrop `bg-black/50`; slide in and out 300ms. `SheetHeader` is `bg-muted/50 p-4 gap-0.5`, `SheetTitle` font-semibold, `SheetDescription` text-sm muted; `SheetFooter` `mt-auto justify-end gap-3 p-4`. Close X top-right at 70% opacity.

**Header:** entity sheets use `SheetFormHeader` (`components/sheets/sheet-form-header.tsx`), not a hand-built `SheetHeader`: one compact title row (`p-2.5`, `text-base`): the entity's icon (the default `leading`; a person shows their avatar), then the title, which is the record's name when editing ("Acme Industries", "Invoice INV-0142", "Hour block for Acme") and "Create <entity>" when creating, never "Edit <entity>"; with its own close button (pass `hideCloseButton` to `SheetContent`) and a 2px bottom rule in the entity's colour (`ENTITY_ACCENTS.<entity>.sheetHeader`, from the entity registry: see **Entities** in the README). Client is a state of a company, not an entity: the Client badge, the Clients filters, the company sheet's Client column (Bill this company) and the client rows on Projects use the client briefcase (`ENTITY_ICONS.client`, `ENTITY_ACCENTS.client`). A sign-in is part of the person and has no icon of its own. No eyebrow or description above the fold. The registry's `icon` class tints an entity's icon in list cells (e.g. the invoice icon on the Invoices list).

**The layout sets the width.** `columns={1}` (720px: fits the full rich text toolbar on one line, with room to spare) for a one-column sheet: person, task, hour block, template, the phone filters (and the project for people who aren't admins). `columns={2}` (768px below `lg`, a set 1184px from `lg`) for a form beside a side column: company, lead, invoice, project (admins: GitHub, Vercel and Supabase on the right), submission (what the visitor sent on the left, emails, attribution and session on the right); the side column takes a named split from `SHEET_SIDE_COLUMN_CLASSES`: `default` (384px, the invoice) or `even` (480px, the lead, whose tasks and updates get worked in as much as the form, the company, whose Client column holds the billing editor and Projects, the project, whose GitHub / Vercel / Supabase rows need the room, and the submission, whose long URLs and ids need it; the form keeps about 700px so the full rich text toolbar still fits on one line). No other widths. The side column stacks below `lg`. Nothing in between, and no per-sheet `max-w-*` override. Phones get the full screen. The one exception is the task sheet's planning panel, which widens the sheet while it's open.

**Focus lands on the first field.** Opening any sheet, create or edit, puts the cursor in its first field so you can just type (a new task: Title); on edit the caret goes to the end of the text. `SheetContent` does it for every sheet: a field marked `data-autofocus` wins, else the first enabled input, textarea, editor or picker trigger outside the header (read-only and disabled fields are skipped). Fields that render after the sheet's data loads take focus when they appear, unless focus has already moved (a click, a dialog).

**Section heading icons.** A section may lead its title with an icon: `SheetSection icon={…}`. An entity's registry icon (Tasks, Time logs, People, Projects, Invoices, Hour blocks) shows in that entity's colour automatically; a non-entity icon stays muted and comes from `CONCEPT_ICONS` (`lib/concept-icons.ts`: comment `MessageCircle`, attachment `Paperclip`, activity `History`), the same icon the task cards use for that concept. Never grey an entity icon by hand. The company and person sheets' sections (People, Leads; Companies) show no heading icon: leave `icon` off there. (guard: review)

**List sections sit tight.** A section that holds a list or an empty state (any section with a +, and Comments) puts its heading 8px above the content, the same gap as a field's label above its control; `SheetSection` does it when it has an `action` or `list`. A section of fields keeps 16px. (guard: review)

**One add button, one related list.** Every sheet section's add action (Add person, Add company, Add line item, Add task, Add update, Log time, Add member, Link repository, Create project) comes from `EntityList` (`components/sheets/entity-list.tsx`, see `entity-list.md`): the + alone, a ghost icon button on the section's title row, its full label the accessible name, with the list and a clickable empty state under it. Never a labelled outline button or a hand-built + beside a section title. (guard: lint)

**No generic first heading.** A sheet's first group of fields has no "Details" heading: it only restates the sheet. Leave `SheetSection`'s `title` out. Headings name real groups (Billing, Commission, People). (guard: eslint `pts/no-details-section`)

**Dividers between sections, except around a few boxed lists.** Sheet sections are separated by a full-width `Separator` in a `gap-6` column. A few EntityList sections drop the line: company People and Leads, person Companies, invoice Line items. Stacked sections with no line between them sit 24px apart, the same as one field to the next (`SHEET_SECTIONS_CLASS`: the company's Company column, the lead's Tasks and Updates). Invoice Line items alone takes `SHEET_BLOCK_CLASS`, 48px around it. The first group of fields never gets a line above it. (guard: review)

**The activity stream is last.** Updates, Comments, and then the collapsed Activity end the sheet: on a two-column sheet they are the last sections of the right column, below projects, tasks and everything else. (guard: review)

**No tabs in a sheet.** Everything is a `SheetSection` in one scroll, or in two columns. **The company sheet is two columns, no tabs:** Company on the left (details, People, Leads, Updates) and Client on the right (`even` split): the **Bill this company** switch is always first. Switched on, the billing fields show under it (the effective month, terms, commission, the save cautions), then Projects as record rows with Create project; switching it on for a company that isn't billed only changes the form (Save makes it a client, on create too); a former client's switch sits under one line with its last billing type ("Former client, last billed Net 30. Billing stopped."). No empty-state panel and no separate Make a client button. Sales sees the Client column read-only (where the company stands, no controls). Old links `?tab=billing`, `?tab=projects` and `?tab=people` open the sheet scrolled to that section; `?tab=details` or none is the top. **The bottom of a sheet is fixed: comments or updates, then Activity, last.** The task sheet runs its fields, Time logs (project tasks only), Comments, then Activity; the lead sheet's side column runs Conversion, Tasks, then Updates.

**Activity is collapsed.** A sheet that shows a record's history uses `SheetActivitySection` (`components/sheets/sheet-activity-section.tsx`), never a hand-built `SheetSection` around `ActivityFeed`: a closed accordion headed "Activity (12)" with a chevron on the house Accordion (`@pts/ui/accordion`, see `accordion.md`). The count is one cheap request (`GET /api/activity?count=1`, the feed's filters and access rules, a `count(*)`); the entries load only when it's opened, and unmount when it's closed. Edit only: a create sheet has no Activity. Today only the task sheet shows one.

**Opening:** a sheet is addressed by one query param (`?task=<uuid>`, `?company=new`) through `useSheetParams`/`useSheetParamSelection` — never a route segment, never a hand-written URL; build links with `lib/sheets/hrefs.ts`. `open` comes from the hook's local mirror, not the raw param.

**Rules**

- Save is always enabled; disable only while saving or on a real validation failure. Never gate on `isDirty`. (guard: eslint `pts/save-not-gated-on-dirty`)
- **Save results are toasts.** Success ("Company saved", "Company created") and failure ("Unable to save company", the reason in the description) show as a toast on every sheet, with no inline error box. The one exception: a successful **Save** (keep open) doesn't toast, since the sheet itself shows the result; its button reads "✓ Saved" for two seconds instead (`announceKeptOpenSave`), and a warning still toasts. A toast never changes or moves once shown: toasts sit above the footer of a sheet that stays open (a failed save, or a sheet stacked under one that closed), and a Save and close success sits in the corner, since its sheet is closing; a failed save keeps the sheet open with its edits. Field errors (required, format) stay under their field (`FormMessage`). (guard: eslint `pts/toast-copy`; review)
- **Two saves.** The footer (`SheetFormFooter`) only saves: undo and redo on the left; on the right **Save** (outline; ⇧⌘S / Ctrl+Shift+S, shown on hover), which saves and keeps the sheet open, then **Save and close** (primary, last, like the portal's dialogs; Enter in a field and ⌘S / Ctrl+S do the same; its hover hint shows ⌘S). The keys come from `SHORTCUTS.save` and `SHORTCUTS.saveAndStay`. On create they read **Create** and **Create and close**. Both run the sheet's one submit function with a `SheetSaveMode` (`close` or `stay`, `lib/sheets/save-mode.ts`), so validation and the stale-save check cover both. While saving, the button that started the save (⌘S counts as Save and close, ⇧⌘S as Save) shows "Saving..." and both are disabled. (guard: review)
- **Undo and redo.** The stack (`useSheetFormControls`) starts at the record as loaded or last saved, never at the blank form the sheet opened with: a load or a save that leaves the form equal to its saved state starts it again; a late field load (`resetField` with a new default) fills that field into every step instead, so edits made while it loaded stay undoable. A load never adds a step: never `setValue` from an effect to fill in or normalize loaded data (seed it in the reset), and set dependent fields in the control's change handler (picking a client sets its tax rate), not an effect that undo would re-run. Every edit is a step, including state kept outside the form (a second form, `useState`): put it on the stack with `useSheetHistoryState`, and keep anything that loads later out of that state (store ids, look names up), so it can join as soon as the control shows. Files are steps too: adding one uploads it at once but only lists it (undo takes it off), and removing one, new or saved, can be undone; an upload no longer listed is deleted when the sheet resets, saves or closes, never while undo could bring it back. (guard: review)
- **⌘Z belongs to the top sheet.** Only the topmost open sheet answers ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z and ⌘S, and it claims ⌘Z even with nothing to undo, so the browser's own text undo never changes a field behind the stack. Focus in a dialog or popover over the sheet, or in an element marked `data-own-undo` (`OWN_UNDO_ATTRIBUTE`: an editor left with its own undo, an inline form that saves on its own click like a lead update), keeps the key. (guard: review)
- **Record actions live in the header's Actions menu** (`SheetRecordMenu`, beside the close X), never in the footer: Duplicate, then Archive (destructive, last), the same list as the record's right-click menu. Only existing records show it. A disabled Archive says why inside the menu. (guard: review)
- **Duplicate** is the first item in that menu, with the `Copy` icon as in the right-click menu, on records that can be copied (task, lead, invoice, hour block) and only for people who can create them. It opens a new create sheet filled in from the record's saved values (`useSheetParams().duplicate`, `?<entity>=copy-<id>`), asking to discard unsaved edits first (`confirmDiscard` from `useSheetLifecycle`). (guard: review)
- **Save and close** clears the entity param, for create and edit alike. (guard: review)
- **Save (keep open)** goes through `useSaveAndStay` (`lib/sheets/use-save-and-stay.ts`): an edit sheet re-baselines to what it saved (closing right after doesn't ask to discard) and reloads the record from the server; a create sheet swaps `new` in the URL for the created id (`useSheetParams().swap`, a history replace) and reloads as that record's edit sheet, with Save disabled until the record arrives so a second press can't create it twice. Never flip a create sheet into edit mode any other way. (guard: review)
- **Say what Save does.** A save side effect (an email sent, a sign-in created, billing started or stopped, someone added to a company) shows as one caution alert, `SaveSideEffect` (see `alert.md`), never a muted line. (guard: review)
- **Only Save writes.** Nothing inside a sheet saves on click, toggle, switch, star, drag or blur: those change the form, and Save applies them. Closing with unsaved changes asks "Discard changes?". (guard: review)
- **Instant actions are their own records or act outside the app**, so they save on their own click: comments, lead updates, logging time (its dialog), invoice email / mark as sent / revert to draft / void, acknowledging a submission, planning (start, generate, dispatch, cancel), and sending a password reset. Sending an invoice is blocked while it has unsaved edits. Uploads (photos, attachments) go to a staging path at once and are claimed on Save. (guard: review)
- Focus lands on the field marked `data-autofocus`, else the popup (not the close button). (guard: review)
- A two-column sheet (form plus a side column, like the lead and invoice sheets) stacks below `lg`: the form, then the side column, in one scroll with the footer pinned to the bottom. Side by side, the form column is too narrow to use under `lg`. (guard: review)
- **Create and edit show the same sheet**, at the same size. Anything that needs the new record's id (a company's people, billing and portal access; a person's sign-in; a task's assignee and attachments) is collected in the form and written in the same Save. Only what can't exist before the record stays edit-only: activity and history, created/updated dates, archive/delete, share links. Instant records (a lead's tasks and updates, a task's time logs and comments) keep their `SheetSection` heading on create over an `EmptyState` ("Add tasks after you save the lead."); a section that can't apply yet (a lead's conversion) stays hidden. (guard: review)
- One Save, one transaction: everything the sheet's form writes (the record plus any company or person it finds or creates) commits together or not at all, and activity rows are written after the commit. (guard: review)
- **No silent overwrites (every sheet).** Save sends the record's `updated_at` as the sheet loaded it (`useSaveConflict`, `lib/sheets/save-conflict/`); the server locks the row (`lockForSave`) and refuses the save if anyone saved since, writing nothing. The refusal opens `SaveConflictDialog`, an alert dialog with the standard footer buttons, written for people who don't read diffs. The title names who changed the record and when ("You saved this company in another tab" when it was you). Each field they changed shows what they saved next to what this sheet holds (the sheet passes `yours={() => form.getValues()}`), with a yellow "You didn't change this. Saving yours puts the old … back." when Save would undo their change; without it, before and after. No word counts, strikethrough or collapsing. Three buttons: **Keep editing**, **Use Kris’s version** (drops your edits, shows the record as it is now) and **Save mine over Kris’s** (destructive; re-sends the same save expecting their version, so a third save in between is caught too). Never a lock: nothing is held while a sheet is open, so nothing can get stuck. Only what a person changes moves the version: the `set_sheet_updated_at()` trigger (migration 0133) ignores machine-written columns (worker status, invoice views, Stripe ids), whose writers must not set `updatedAt`, and every sheet save stamps `updatedAt` so a save of related rows alone still counts. (guard: review)
- **Who else is here.** `SheetPresence` goes in `SheetFormHeader`'s `presence` slot on every edit sheet. It shows as one yellow (warning) alert above the first field, easing in when someone arrives and out when the last one leaves (`InlineReveal`; the slot cancels the form's gap so nothing jumps) (the header keeps the title and Actions alone): their avatars and "Kris Crawford has this company open" or "Kris Crawford is editing this company" (they have unsaved edits). It is Supabase Realtime presence on a channel named by an HMAC of the record under a staff-only key (`lib/sheets/presence/`), so a closed tab leaves within seconds and nothing is stored. Supabase closes a channel that sends more than 5 presence updates in 30 seconds, so the editing flag goes out at most every 8 seconds (`presence-pacer.ts`), and a tab whose channel the server closes rejoins by itself. When someone saves, a clean sheet loads their save on its own; a sheet with edits keeps them, and its next Save opens the conflict dialog. Pass the sheet's full dirty flag (attachments, repos), never just the form's. (guard: review)
- **Restore lives on the archive list only.** A sheet never offers Restore. An archived record opened in a sheet is read-only, with one muted line at the top ("Archived. Restore it from the archive list.") and no archive action in its Actions menu. (guard: review)
- Presses inside toasts or other popups (select, dropdown) don't count as outside presses. (guard: review)
- **Related records stack.** Opening a person, company, lead or task from inside a sheet opens its own sheet on top (the URL keeps the stack order, `?company=…&contact=…`); closing it goes back to the sheet underneath. Any record can stack, two of one kind included (company A, a person, then company B: `?company=A&contact=…&company=B`). The one exception: a record already open in the stack is never opened twice. Clicking it goes back to it by closing the sheets above, each asking first if it has unsaved changes, as its own Close would (a paid invoice, its hour block, then the invoice link in the hour block closes the hour block). A click on the sheet you're on does nothing. This holds for every way to open a sheet (links, record rows, Open buttons, menus, Create and Duplicate), because they all go through `open`; the rules live in `lib/sheets/stack.ts`. Open it with `useSheetParams().open(entity, id)`; to land on a section (the company sheet's billing), call `setSheetSectionTarget('company', id, 'billing')` first. Sheets have no tabs and no `tab=` param. Never with a `*SheetHref` link or `router.push`: those leave the page. Never navigate away or replace the sheet you're in; a link to a page (a project board, the update composer) is the only way out. A page can open the same way (winning a lead on the board opens its company's sheet at its billing over the board). Small focused tasks (link a repo, email an invoice, log time) may open a dialog over a sheet. (guard: review)
- **Linked records are rows.** Every record listed in a sheet (a company's people, leads and projects, a converted lead's company, person and projects, an hour block's invoice, an invoice's hour blocks) is a `SheetRecordRow` from `components/sheets/entity-record-rows.tsx`: `ContactRecordRow`, `CompanyRecordRow`, `LeadRecordRow`, `ProjectRecordRow`, `InvoiceRecordRow`, `HourBlockRecordRow`, inside a `SheetRecordList`. A row with a sheet opens it on top (`onOpen`); a project row links to its board (`href`), and one without a slug is plain text. Callers add only a note and actions. (guard: review)
- **Record rows with fields (label and value).** A record row that holds form fields (a person's title and portal access in the company sheet's People section, a person's title at each company in the person sheet's Companies list, a closer's share in the company's Client column) keeps its first line as the normal preview (entity icon in its colour, name and detail line, row actions), then a hairline, then one label / value row per field: pass them as `fields`, each a `RecordRowField` (`components/sheets/sheet-record-row.tsx`). Labels sit in one 112px column so they line up from card to card; values start on the same edge. Text fields use `RECORD_ROW_INPUT_CLASS`: they read as plain text until hovered or focused, when the input box appears; never a boxed input inside the boxed card. A text field takes `grow` (fills the value column, up to 288px); a switch or short input keeps its own width. Every row is 32px tall. A field's visible label is its label: no placeholder standing in for it (a placeholder like "Add title" may show the empty state); keep an `aria-label` that names the record ("Portal access for Ada Lovelace"); no status tooltip on a switch. (guard: review)
- **Person sign-in (person sheet).** Works like a company's Client section. The **User** section (the sign-in entity's icon, the key) has a **Can sign in** switch at the right of its heading, switch first; a live sign-in adds **Send reset link** (instant, small outline button) 16px to the switch's left. Switching it on for someone with no sign-in shows a `SaveSideEffect` naming what Save does ("Saving creates a sign-in for Ada Lovelace at ada@acme.com and emails them a link to the client portal."); it needs an email (the switch is disabled, with the reason, until there is one). Once on, plain fields, as in the Client section (the sign-in is a state of the person, not a record, so no record card): form fields two to a row, full width, like the Client section's billing fields: **Signs in as** (the email, disabled with a tooltip saying to change it in Email above) with "Last sign-in: …" under it, then **Role** (Admin, Client, Sales, as `RoleBadge`s, always all three: Admin and Sales put them at the staff company, Client takes them off it on Save, with a caution saying so), and **Role starts** for a role change, with the month it counts from under it. Switching a live sign-in off disables it on Save ("Ada Lovelace won’t be able to sign in once you save."); back on re-enables it. Which client companies a Client sees is each company card's **Portal access** switch, the same setting as the company sheet's People cards, both drawn by `PortalAccessField` (`components/companies/portal-access-control.tsx`). Without a live Client sign-in it is off and locked (a stored "on" isn't cleared) with one muted line under it, "Needs a sign-in with the Client role.", right of the switch, and the way to fix it: "Turn on Can sign in below." on the person sheet (the switch is just below), **Open person** on the company sheet (stacks their sheet). No screen makes a sign-in as a side effect. Sales see "Only an admin can see or change someone’s sign-in." Everything saves with the sheet's Save; the invite goes out after it. `contact-access-section.tsx`; `/design#access`. (guard: review)
