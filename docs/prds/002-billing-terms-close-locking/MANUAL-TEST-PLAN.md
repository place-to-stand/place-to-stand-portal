## 🟥 Section A — Highest priority (failed/flaky/observed issues)

- [ ] **A1. Date-field keyboard entry (ties to 05.1/05.3).** The time-log date picker's month/day segments ignored synthetic keystrokes during automation — the segments *displayed* new values but the form submitted today's date (the first attempt logged Aug 7 instead of Jul 15). Hand-check: open Add time log, set a past date **by keyboard** and by clicking the segments, save, and confirm the row shows the date you set. If real typing also fails, this is a genuine date-picker bug (pre-existing component, but the closed-month warnings depend on dates being settable).
- [ ] **A2. Legacy clients without origination/closer can't change billing type (ties to 01.5).** The client sheet requires origination + closer before any save — Acme Test Co (pre-PRD fixture) silently blocked the billing-type change until both were set. Decide whether that's acceptable for your real book (every prod client presumably has partners set; if not, you'll hit this when switching them to prepaid).
- [ ] **A3. Toast stacking on prepaid overage (ties to 05.1).** Logging time for a prepaid client who's out of block hours triggers the "Log anyway" overage confirm, then the success toast, then the closed-month warning toast. It behaved correctly, but eyeball whether three stacked prompts feels acceptable in the common late-logging flow.
